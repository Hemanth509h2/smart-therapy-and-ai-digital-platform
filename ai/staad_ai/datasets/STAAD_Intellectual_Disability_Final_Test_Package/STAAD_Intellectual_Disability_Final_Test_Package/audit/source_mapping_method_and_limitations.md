# Source mapping method and limitations

Each knowledge record is linked to one or more public professional/clinical sources and a topic or recommendation locator. Mapping is provisional and based on source-topic alignment; it is not sentence-by-sentence verification. No record is labeled clinically validated. The source register notes the role and limitation of each source. Three records (ID-KB-039 to ID-KB-041) are STAAD engineering/safety controls informed by the clinical context rather than direct clinical claims. A qualified reviewer should verify the full source text, update locators, and approve or reject every record before clinical use.

## Jurisdiction note
NICE and NHS sources are UK sources. Their recommendations can inform design but legal rights, service eligibility, consent, safeguarding, and care pathways must be checked against the deployment jurisdiction, including India if relevant.
